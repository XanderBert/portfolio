In the first semester of my last year at Digital Arts And Entertainment we had a group project where we needed to create a game in a few months.

**Liquid Gravity is a fast-paced movement-shooter where you skate, control gravity, and fight, move faster and more stylishly to deal more damage and stay alive.**

The game features:

- **Gravity-based movement:** ground is relative! Skate on the ceiling or the walls, the world is your playground.
- **Arcade-style fast-paced gameplay:** inspired by old-time classics which prioritize gameplay over theatrics. The combat is mostly pounce and hide: strike in, do as much damage as possible, and leave to make enemies lose track.
- **Clean, bulky sci-fi art style:** big, bulky shapes with clean, distinct silhouettes, appealing from all angles.
- **In-depth scoring and combo systems:** easy to pick up but devilishly hard to master. Your final score combines a number of factors, and you must master all the skills to earn an illustrious S Rank.

https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HZMQZbEAEXM

## What did I do?

I was the technical lead and lead programmer on the project. During prototyping, I designed the systems and evaluated tooling options, then built the custom gravity solution the game is built around. In the final stretch, I focused on profiling and optimizing shaders and game code to keep performance stable on our target hardware.
